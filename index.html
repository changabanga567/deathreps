How it works

Small, click-through, out of your way. Only shows up after you die.

1 · YOU DIEA small card appears in the corner with the exercise for whatever killed you.
2 · DO THE REPSAn animated figure and a form tip show you how. Reps fit the time before the next round.
3 · CONFIRMPress a hotkey when you're done. Finish before the round starts for bonus XP.
4 · LEVEL UPEarn XP, combos and badges. Get a grade after every match.

Privacy & fair play

What DeathReps uses

DeathReps only uses Overwolf's official Game Events for VALORANT: your own death and kill-feed entry, the round phase, and match start/end. It does not use the Riot Games API.

Fair play

DeathReps does not read game memory, automate input, or show any information you don't already have. It gives no competitive advantage and only reacts after your own death.

Your data

Your settings, XP and workout log are stored locally on your PC. DeathReps has no accounts and doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. Ads in the desktop window are served by Overwolf under the Overwolf Privacy Policy.

Health

DeathReps is for general fitness and fun, not medical advice. Warm up, use good form, and stop if anything hurts. Only use a chair that doesn't roll for chair exercises.
